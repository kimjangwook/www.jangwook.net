Before evaluating live captions

These materials are for educational use. Recording playback and the computer-generated demo are not presented as actual live broadcasts. The observation records in the cases below are hypothetical, not actual broadcast audit findings.

Screens to compare directly

  • Recorded video and caption playback: the playback time differs from the recording time.
  • Demo with code-generated visuals and sound: even when generated now, it is not a live broadcast of a real-world event.

Hypothetical broadcast observation cases

For an actual broadcast, transmission records and viewer screens must be used to verify these premises. A scenario alone does not establish whether an actual broadcast meets the criterion.

L01 · Hypothetical broadcast with complete captions displayed

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

Assume that the original audio and records of actual caption display have been compared over the entire 40-second duration using the same recipient clock. The captions include dialogue, speakers, and important warning tones, and there are no additional sounds.

L02 · Captions with altered negation

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

The original audio says “Do not discard it before saving,” but the displayed captions say “Discard it before saving.” Assume there is no other caption path that corrects this.

L03 · Statements by two off-screen panelists

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

Panelist A says it is acceptable to proceed with the update, while panelist B says not to proceed. Assume their voices are distinguishable in the original audio, but neither the captions nor the screen provides clues linking each statement to A or B.

L04 · Captions include dialogue but omit warning tones

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

Assume dialogue and speakers are displayed, but the captions do not include information about the two warning tones indicating authentication failure.

L05 · Instruction displayed after the decision screen closes

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

The original audio and the relevant decision screen occur at 14–22 seconds. Assume the caption “Do not discard it before saving” first appears at 30–38 seconds, on the next task screen, and no other alternative is available.

L06 · Only server generation logs remain

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

An event list for the original audio and caption generation logs are available, but delivery to and display on the user’s screen have not been observed. Assume the clock offset between the two logs is also unknown.

L07 · One screen image showing captions

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

Only one caption line at the 20-second point of the 40-second duration is provided. There are no records of the original audio, timeline, other segments, or other display paths.

L08 · Open captions displayed instead of closed captions

Evidence type: hypothetical observation

Assume closed captions are not displayed, but open captions on the same screen accurately convey dialogue, speakers, and important sounds in synchronized form throughout the entire duration.

L09 · Page playing an existing recording
