# Visual identity
Use the existing jangwook.net quiet modern knowledge-library design. Educational wireframes, restrained motion, readable controls and conditions. No advertising hero, gradients or decorative branding.
## Colors
paper #fafaf8; surface #f1f1ee; ink #252525; muted #62625e; rule #deded9.
## Typography
Noto Sans KR with system CJK fallbacks, one sans family matching the site. 52px headings, 30px explanation and controls.
## Motion
Short 0.4 second scene crossfades; subtle entrances. Hold each step to read. No flashing, music, narration or autoplay.
## What NOT to Do
No cobalt blue, neon, AI gradients, marketing, invented real accounts, or rendering actual recovery codes.
